## model coronal emission spectrum of TW Hya
The X-ray and EUV spectrum data of TW Hya presented by Shoda, Nakatani, and Takasao (2025)

**XUV_spectrum.csv**  
spectral energy flux of TW Hya measured at a distance of 1 astronomical unit. The first column lists wavelengths (in ${\rm Å}$), while the second column shows energy flux per unit wavelength (in ${\rm erg \ cm^{-2} \ s^{-1} \ Å^{-1}}$). Note that although data is available up to 1200 ${\rm Å}$, reliable values are limited to those below the Lyman edge (912 ${\rm Å}$) (see Figure 5 in [Shoda & Takasao 2021 Astronomy & Astrophysics](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/abs/2021/12/aa41563-21/aa41563-21.html).).

**dem.csv**  
The data for the differential emission measure used to calculate the spectrum is provided. The first column represents the base-10 logarithm of temperature (in ${\rm K}$), while the second column indicates the DEM in cgs units (in ${\rm cm^{-5} \ K^{-1}}$).
