## model coronal emission spectrum of TW Hya and associated data
The X-ray and EUV spectrum data of TW Hya presented by Shoda, Nakatani, and Takasao (2025)

XUV_spectrum.csv contains the spectral energy flux of TW Hya measured at a distance of 1 astronomical unit. The first column lists wavelengths (in Angstrom), while the second column shows energy flux per unit wavelength (in erg/cm²/s/Å). Note that although data is available up to 1200 Å, reliable values are limited to those below the Lyman edge (912 Å).
